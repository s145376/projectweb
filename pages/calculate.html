Why this calculator?

STM helps students plan their time. This page turns your courses and deadlines into the number of study hours you need, so you can compare it with the hours you really have and set a realistic weekly goal.

The formula

  1. Course hours = credit hours × 2
  2. Assignment hours = assignments × 1.5
  3. Exam hours = exams × 4
  4. Project hours = 4 (only if you have a group project)
  5. Subtotal = sum of the four lines above
  6. Total = subtotal × difficulty factor (easy 0.9, medium 1.0, hard 1.2)
  7. Hours per day = total ÷ study days

The result

  • If total is more than your available hours: overloaded.
  • If total is 80% or more of your available hours: busy.
  • Otherwise: light week.
