Frequently asked questions

Is my data private?

Yes. STM only asks for a display name and an e-mail address. Passwords are stored as BCRYPT hashes and all traffic uses HTTPS.

How do I change when I get reminders?

Go to Profile & Settings and choose a lead time (2 hours to 1 week) and your quiet hours.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the contact form above and choose "Technical problem". We will help you reset your password.

Can I track tasks that are not related to a course?

Yes. The General workspace is made for personal, religious, wellness and club tasks.

Does STM cost anything?

No. STM is free for all students.

STM helps students organise courses, deadlines, goals and personal commitments in one calm, distraction-free place.
