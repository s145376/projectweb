Tips for good goals

  1. Specific – "Study 15 hours", not "study more".
  2. Measurable – give it a number and a unit.
  3. Time-bound – always set a deadline.
  4. Balanced – mix academic, personal and wellness goals.

Small daily steps beat last-minute marathons.
