About STM

STM – Student Task Management is a web platform that helps every student organise academic work and personal commitments in one calm, distraction-free place.

Create a free account Log in

Our mission

Students often juggle four or five courses at once, each with different assignment formats, quizzes and project deadlines. Paper planners and scattered note apps make it easy to miss a short homework or forget an exam date. STM brings everything together: tasks are organised by semester and course, the dashboard shows only what matters today, and progress bars turn hard work into visible results.

We also believe student life is more than coursework. STM gives the same attention to religious commitments, wellness and club activities, so students can balance their values and their studies without switching between apps.

What STM offers

  • Academic and general workspaces
  • Create, edit and delete tasks with deadlines and priorities
  • A "Focus today" list for daily priorities
  • Deadline reminders with lead times you choose
  • Clear completed / not-completed status tracking
  • A colour-coded month calendar and weekly timetable
  • Semester and weekly goals with progress bars

How it works

Getting organised takes about five minutes.

  1. Sign up with your e-mail and a display name.
  2. Create your semester and add your courses with colour tags.
  3. Add tasks – assignments, exams, projects or personal tasks – with due dates.
  4. Check "Focus today" each morning and tick tasks off as you finish them.
  5. Review your goals every week and adjust your plan.
